Privacy Policy

Pandy: Voice Diary · last updated 14 November 2026

We collect no personal data. Your recordings, transcripts, diary pages, moods and Pandy's questions are made and kept on your iPhone, and they never leave it. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Pandy Diary is built and maintained by Merve Kavaklıoğlu ("we"). We don't run any servers, and the app:

  • does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts;
  • does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs;
  • does not send your recordings, transcripts or diary pages to us or to any AI service, Apple's Private Cloud Compute included.

How your diary is made

  • Recording: the microphone is used only while you record. Entries recorded on Apple Watch go straight to your own iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity.
  • Transcription: speech is turned into text on your device, with Apple's speech recognition and the open-source Whisper model running locally on your iPhone. No OpenAI service is contacted.
  • Diary pages, moods and weekly reflections: written on your device by Apple Intelligence's on-device model.
  • Pandy's questions: written on your device from your recent pages, and kept on your iPhone.
  • Translation, when needed, uses Apple's on-device Translation.

When the app connects to the internet

  • Once, to download its speech model (about 626 MB) from Hugging Face. It's a plain file download: nothing about you or your diary is sent.
  • When iOS downloads Apple's own speech, translation and Apple Intelligence files.
  • For the free trial and the one-time purchase, handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

Your recordings, transcripts, pages, moods, feelings, topics, weekly reflections and Pandy's questions are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. They are removed when you delete an entry or the app. You can lock the diary with Face ID; the app never sees your face data, iOS only tells it whether unlocking worked. The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your iPhone; while the Face ID lock is on, it doesn't show Pandy's question on the lock screen. If you choose to share a page or export your diary, it goes only where you send it, and exported text is no longer protected by the app's lock.

Children

Pandy Diary is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version ever starts collecting data, this page will be updated and the App Store release notes will say so. We will not start collecting data without telling you.
