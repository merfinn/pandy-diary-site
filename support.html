Getting started

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Titles, moods, highlights, weekly reflections and Pandy's questions need Apple Intelligence (turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). Without it, your entries are kept as you said them, and their pages are written automatically once Apple Intelligence is ready.

Why does the app download something on first launch?

Pandy Diary downloads its speech recognition model once (about 626 MB), so it can understand about 100 languages right on your iPhone. Wi-Fi is recommended. You can already record while it downloads; your entry is written down once it's done. Nothing about you is uploaded.

Pandy's questions

Where do Pandy's questions come from?

From your last few pages. Pandy looks for plans, worries and things you were waiting for, and asks how they turned out, with your own sentence shown underneath. It's all done on your iPhone. Tap "Different question" to see the next one, or "Skip" to talk about anything. When there's nothing to follow up, Pandy asks an everyday question instead.

Do I have to answer the question?

No. Talk about anything you like; the question is only an invitation.

Can I stop Pandy asking about something?

Yes. Open Threads on the home screen. It lists everything Pandy may ask about; turn off anything you'd rather not talk about, or choose "Ask this next time".

The evening ritual

What does "Close today" do?

It marks the day as written, so the home screen shows "Today is written." It's only a gentle marker: you can still add more entries afterwards. Until 4 in the morning, an entry still counts for the day before.

Can I change when the evening starts?

Yes. In Settings, "Close the day from" sets when Pandy starts asking if you're ready to close the day, and "Evening reminder" sets the time of the daily notification.

I missed a day

That's okay, there are no streaks to lose. The next day Pandy offers "Add a note for yesterday".

Entries

"I couldn't hear that": my entry wasn't understood

The recording was too quiet, too noisy or too short to understand reliably, so Pandy doesn't guess. Check your spoken languages in Settings, then tap "Record again" a little closer to the microphone, or "Keep the recording".

Can I change the mood or the page?

Yes. Tap another face to change the mood; your choice is kept, even if the page is written again. To change the title, the text or the moments worth keeping, open the page and choose "Edit page".

Can I get my diary out of the app?

Yes. In Settings, "Export diary" shares all your pages and quick notes as text, wherever you choose.

Recordings from Apple Watch don't appear

Open Pandy Diary on your iPhone. Watch recordings are sent to the iPhone over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and are written down there; the watch shows how many are still waiting.

Purchase

How does the free trial work?

Tap "Start free trial" to use everything free for 7 days. There's no subscription and nothing renews: after the trial, you can keep Pandy Diary with a single one-time purchase. Your existing pages always stay readable.

I bought it on another device

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Privacy

Is my diary uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is made and stored on your iPhone; there are no accounts or servers. See the Privacy Policy.
